Privacy policy

Beth-Nahrin Mail Backup is a private utility used by Beth-Nahrin to back up its email service. Contact: info@bethnahrin.se.

The utility uses authorized Google Drive access to create, read, update and delete its own backup files, including when removing backups that have expired. It stores encrypted copies of mailbox data and recovery configuration in the authorized Google Drive account. It does not use Google user data for advertising or sell it.

Google Drive authorization credentials are stored on the backup server to allow scheduled backups. The account owner can revoke access through their Google Account. Revoking access stops future backups; existing encrypted backups remain in Drive until deleted. The account owner can contact us to ask about or request deletion of the backup files.
